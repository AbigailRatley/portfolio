Cherry Cork Studios

My main interest and focus for my career is website design and development. Therefore, for my self-led major project in my final year of university I decided to choose a client led branding and web development project; so I found a startup and offered to create all of their branding materials for them for free. I created all of the brand design, graphics, photography and full stack web development. I am majoritively self taught by using multiple advanced readings and learning to combine HTML and Javascript with CSS and PHP. By the end of this project, I had taken a company that had no branding at all (logo, social media, etc) to a business with branding guidelines including font and colour specifications, logos and social media guidelines with professional grade photography; and multiple online presences including a website with multiple pages that I hand coded.
